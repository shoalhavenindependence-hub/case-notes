Privacy Policy

Shift Case Notes · Last updated 15 October 2026

Shift Case Notes is a note-taking app for support workers. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

Your notes stay with you

Client names and case notes you enter are stored on your device, and in your own Google Drive if you turn on Google Drive sync. We do not run any servers that receive, store or read your notes, and we have no way to see them. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the notes on your phone are deleted, but any copy already sent to your Google Drive stays there.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you tap Connect Google Drive, you sign in with your Google account and allow the app to create and update its own files in your Google Drive. The app then copies your clients and case notes, with their dates and times, into a "Shift Case Notes" folder there, with a spreadsheet for each client. Your notes travel directly from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection and are never sent to us.

The app can only see and change the files it created itself. It cannot see any other files in your Google Drive. It uses this access only to keep those spreadsheets up to date for you, and never to advertise, sell, or share your data with anyone.

Shift Case Notes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can stop sync at any time with Disconnect in the Clients tab, and remove the app's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Dictation and the microphone

The app asks to use your microphone only when you tap Dictate. Speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (usually provided by Google). That service may process the audio under its own privacy policy. The app does not record or keep any audio.

Purchases

The app is free for 30 days, then a one-off purchase unlocks it. Payments are handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but never your card or payment details.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts with us (Google sign-in is only used to reach your own Google Drive)
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No location, contacts or other personal data

Children

The app is intended for adults working in support roles and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
